LEGAL.01 / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what information EugenIX processes when you use account features, save resources, or send a message through the website.

Effective 25 August 2026EUGENIX.DEV

01 / SCOPE

About this policy

EugenIX is the personal software engineering portfolio and resource hub of Eugene Eyambe Ndeme. This policy applies to information processed through eugenix.dev, including its Resource Library, account features, saved resources, and contact form.

02 / INFORMATION

Information involved

  • When you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase Auth provide the account information needed to authenticate you. Depending on the provider response, this may include your name, email address, provider account identifier, and profile information.
  • When you save a resource, EugenIX associates the saved-resource record with your authenticated account so it can be retrieved later.
  • When you use the contact form, EugenIX processes the name, email address, inquiry type, subject, and message you submit.
  • Hosting, authentication, and delivery infrastructure may process standard request, device, browser, error, and security information needed to operate and protect their services.

03 / USE

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, maintain signed-in sessions, provide saved-resource functionality, respond to messages, deliver contact submissions, diagnose service problems, and protect the website and its supporting services.

EugenIX does not claim to sell users' personal information.

04 / SERVICES

Service infrastructure

Supabase provides authentication and database infrastructure used for account and saved-resource features. Google provides the optional authentication method. Netlify hosts the production website and runs the contact endpoint. Contact submissions are delivered through Resend.

These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Choosing Google authentication redirects you into services operated by Google and Supabase.

05 / CHOICES

Your choices

  • You can browse public portfolio and resource content without signing in.
  • You can choose whether to use Google authentication and saved-resource features.
  • You can sign out through the Resource Library interface.
  • You can contact Eugene to ask about account information or saved-resource data associated with your account.
  • You can send an email directly instead of using the website contact form.

06 / LINKS

External websites

EugenIX links to external websites and services. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies, not this policy.

07 / UPDATES

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the website or its supporting services change. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

08 / CONTACT

Contact

Questions about this policy or account information can be sent to ndemeeugene237@gmail.com.
