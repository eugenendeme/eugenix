CASE.01 / PRODUCTIVITY PRODUCT

Notch.

In development · approximately 30%

A personal planning product intended to make work easier to organize and progress easier to see.

Role
Product development and implementation
Implemented
Personal planning functionality
Planned
Team and freelance workflows

01 / OVERVIEW

A focused planning core.

Notch explores a clearer way to plan work, stay focused, and track progress. The current project is not a completed productivity platform. Its implemented scope is personal planning.

Current boundary: personal planning works today. Broader team and freelance workflows have not shipped.

02 / PROBLEM

Make planning easier to follow.

The product is centered on a practical problem: helping an individual organize work and understand progress without mixing the current experience with a larger future collaboration system.

The public record deliberately separates what can be used now from what remains an intended direction.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION RECORD

What exists today.

Implemented

  • Personal planning functionality
  • A product experience focused on planning work and tracking progress

Not yet implemented

  • Team workflows
  • Freelance workflows
  • A completed or production-deployed product

The repository does not establish a public technology stack for Notch, so no unsupported technology badges are shown here.

04 / CURRENT STATE

Approximately 30% complete.

The percentage is a high-level maturity statement, not a delivery forecast. The useful evidence is the implemented personal-planning scope and the explicit boundary around future collaboration features.

Further work remains before Notch could be described as complete, launched, or ready for team use.
